The chain uses browser Web Crypto SHA-256. Each entry stores its inherited predecessor hash and the predecessor interpretation of the same target, the snapshot's prior record count, the new classification, and its local recording time. Classification never makes a claim factually true. Later reports cannot be used to retroactively alter what a prior reading inherited. A Git-tracked root anchors the starting point; new browser entries are not publicly anchored until exported and committed.
