## Final sweep — 2026-11-08

- [Open the interactive alchemical synthesis](divine-comedy-final-sweep.html)
- Textual scope: **100 cantos** = Inferno **34** + Purgatorio **33** + Paradiso **33**.
- The primary interpretive framework in Dante remains Christian theology: free will, love, justice, purification, grace and contemplation.
- The overlay **Nigredo → Albedo → Rubedo** is a modern alchemical reading, not an assertion that Dante designed a literal laboratory recipe or chemical transmutation.
- **Nigredo / Inferno:** dark wood, downward diagnosis, frozen Lucifer, and reversal of orientation by crossing the Earth's center.
- **Albedo / Purgatorio:** seven penitential terraces, reordering of love, passage through fire, Lethe and Eunoë, renewal of agency.
- **Rubedo / Paradiso:** ascent from the Moon to the Empyrean, faith/hope/love examined, the river of light and celestial rose, Bernard's prayer, three circles and final alignment of will and desire.
- **Most informative hinge:** the bottom of Hell changes from the lowest descent to the beginning of ascent, without replacing the identity of the pilgrim.
- **Strongest textual basis:** `Purgatorio` XVII organizes sins through wrongly directed, insufficient or excessive love.
- The last boundary is the limit of representation; Dante's mystical vision is not fully captured by formulas.
- The companion element cipher stays corrected: `..||..|....||` = 8 dots × 1 + 5 bars × 2 = **18**, corresponding to argon's atomic number. This is a user-defined symbolic mapping; the periodic table is not claimed as Dante's subject.
- The existing project motifs 8 chromatic vertices, 24 witnesses and 1 center are interpretive visualization choices, not canonical numeric counts in the poem.
