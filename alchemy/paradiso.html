Today's encounter

Beatrice answers the vow question by distinguishing a binding free gift of will from the particular promised act, which may under proper authority be exchanged for greater worth. She warns against rash pledges with Jephthah and Agamemnon. Dante and Beatrice rise into Mercury, where shining souls hurry to welcome them. Their first speaker is not named until Canto VI.
