Today's encounter

Dante is caught between two doubts. Beatrice says all blessed souls dwell in the Empyrean, appearing in heavenly spheres to help him understand their differing blessedness. She then distinguishes absolute will, which inwardly resists wrongdoing, from conditioned will, which may yield under fear. Dante asks whether good works can compensate for a broken vow; the answer awaits Canto V.
