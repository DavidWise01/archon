Today's encounter

Dante and Beatrice enter the lunar sphere and see bright and dark markings. Dante suggests differing density explains them. Beatrice tests the account with two near mirrors, one distant mirror, and a lamp, then offers the poem's theological explanation: varying celestial powers and formal qualities, not mere rarity of matter.
