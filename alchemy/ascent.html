Today's encounter

Following Matelda along the stream, Dante sees seven glowing candlesticks and twenty-four white-robed elders. Four many-eyed winged creatures surround a two-wheeled chariot drawn by a griffin. Three women dance by one wheel and four by the other, followed by other symbolic figures. The procession pauses; Beatrice and the water rites still await.
