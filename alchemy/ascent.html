Today's encounter

Statius approaches and explains the tremor: when a soul has completed purification and freely wishes to ascend, the mountain shakes and all souls sing. Statius himself was that soul. He praises the poetry of Virgil, unaware that Virgil is standing beside him; Dante's smile gives the secret away.
