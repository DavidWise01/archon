Today's encounter

Dante enters the Earthly Paradise: a fragrant forest with birdsong and a gentle breeze. A clear stream stops him. Across the water Matelda gathers flowers and sings. She explains the garden's wind and spring and distinguishes Lethe, which removes remembrance of sin, from Eunoe, which restores memory of good deeds. Dante does not cross or drink yet.
