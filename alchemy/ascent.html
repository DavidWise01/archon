Today's encounter

On the fifth terrace, the penitents recall Mary and Fabricius, examples of poverty and honesty. Hugh Capet condemns the greed and violence of his royal descendants and recounts greed punished. Then the mountain shakes and a great Gloria rises. Dante hears it but does not yet know why the earth moved.
