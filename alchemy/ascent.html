Today's encounter

An angel commands Dante to cross the final fire. He hesitates until Virgil invokes Beatrice, then enters the flames with Virgil and Statius. They rest at sunset; Dante dreams of Leah weaving flowers and Rachel contemplating. At dawn Virgil declares Dante's will free and crowns and mitres him over himself.
