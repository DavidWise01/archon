Today's encounter

Dante dreams that an eagle lifts him into fire. When he awakens, Virgil explains that Lucia carried him higher while he slept. Before the gate lie three steps: white, dark and cracked, and red. An angel cuts seven P marks upon Dante's brow and opens the gate with two keys. Inside, a hymn rises.
