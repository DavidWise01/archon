Today's encounter

The second terrace is plain and bare. Unseen voices proclaim generosity. Dante finds souls in gray cloaks whose eyelids are sewn shut; they lean together and pray. Sapia of Siena admits she rejoiced when her own people were defeated, and asks to be remembered.
