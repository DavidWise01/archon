Today's encounter

On the sixth terrace, hunger has hollowed the penitents' faces while fragrant fruit and water remain out of reach. Dante hears a familiar voice and recognizes Forese Donati, though his appearance has changed. Forese credits his wife Nella's prayers with hastening his progress and speaks critically of Florence. The friends walk onward.
