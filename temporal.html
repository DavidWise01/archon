Each report is SHA-256 linked to the previous global report and its own A/B/C station predecessor, and references a verified Bound 012 source event or the committed genesis. ISO timestamps require an explicit timezone. The browser's recording time is client-reported and is not independently authenticated. Future or inconsistent time claims are flagged, not silently corrected. Records are local until exported and subsequently committed to a trusted external anchor.
