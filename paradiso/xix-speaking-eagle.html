The Speaking Eagle

Thirty-five letters have become an eagle. The eagle is woven from many blessed souls, yet it speaks as “I”, not “we.”

Dante asks how divine justice can be understood by a finite human mind. What of a virtuous person who never heard the Christian faith? The eagle's answer is not a simple table of exceptions. It first exposes the limits of the questioner's measure.

Who are you to sit upon the judgment seat, seeing only a handspan?

Its image is the sea: a person on the shore cannot see its bottom, but the depth exists whether the observer sees it or not.

The boundary

A thousand points may speak together without any one point becoming the whole. A shared voice is not identical to perfect knowledge.

The warning to rulers

The eagle condemns kings whose public titles and religious claims cannot conceal unjust conduct. Justice is not a costume. Authority does not make a judgment true.
