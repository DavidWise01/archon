The Order of the Rose

Bernard now teaches Dante the architecture of the celestial rose. Mary sits highest, followed by Eve, Rachel and Beatrice, Sarah, Rebecca, Judith and Ruth. On the opposite dividing line sit John the Baptist, Francis, Benedict and Augustine.

One side contains souls who awaited Christ, the other those who believed after his coming. The lower ranks contain blessed children. The order expresses Dante's theology of grace and history, not a human scorecard of achievement.

One rose; distinct histories; every seat belongs to the whole.
