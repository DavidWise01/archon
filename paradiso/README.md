## Provenance and completeness

This ledger summarizes prior chat chapters. Earlier interactive HTML files (IX–XVIII) were generated as conversation artifacts, but **they are not claimed as committed to this repository**. Their original bytes must be recovered and checked before any exact archival claims. Cantos XIX–XXXIII have been independently committed and verified through the GitHub connector.

Symbolic geometry (8 vertices, 24 witnesses, 1 center) is an interpretive model, not a literal translation of Dante's numerical cosmology or a physical quantum dot simulation.

## Completion
Paradiso XXXIII completes Dante's ascent through the Divine Comedy. The individual chapter artifacts for XIX–XXXIII were committed to this repository; earlier chapters remain a separate archival recovery task. Next natural project boundary: verify the complete corpus, recover missing chapters, and build a navigable append-only index. No further canonical Paradiso cantos follow XXXIII.
