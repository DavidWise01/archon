Paradiso XXIX · Primum Mobile · Natural Boundary 029

Creation and the Angels

For a moment Beatrice remains silent. Then she tells Dante that God created the angels not from need, but as an expression of goodness. In Dante's theological vision, the angels, the heavenly spheres, and matter were created together, outside any long interval of waiting.

Some angels promptly turned away through pride. The faithful angels turned toward divine goodness and were strengthened in understanding and love. The distinction is one of free choice, not simply location within a ring.

Order is not obedience without freedom. The choice matters.

Beatrice then rebukes teachers who substitute invention, vanity, and spectacle for truthful instruction. A persuasive story is not evidence merely because it pleases an audience.
