Each report records a verified successor receipt, the exact source knowledge record within that receipt's inherited prefix, source text, observer channel, observation, evidence basis, and its global and observer-channel predecessor hashes. A new report can differ from every predecessor without altering them. Comparisons use literal strings, not a model's judgment of factual equivalence. History is browser-local until exported and externally anchored; SHA-256 alone neither authenticates an observer nor verifies the observation's truth.
