THE REVERSAL DIALECT

The orientation of a delimiter is part of its meaning. Forward forms and their directional inverses are separate operations; reversing one does not silently execute the other. The six forms below are the authored ARCHON dialect. Here we verify their exact textual encoding and inversion, not physical compression or quantum state change.

[compression]COMPRESSION

Forward square gate; represents compressing into a held representation.

]decompression[DECOMPRESSION

Inverse square gate; represents decompression or expansion.

{brace}BRACE

Forward curly boundary; represents binding or holding.

}unbrace{UNBRACE

Inverse curly boundary; represents explicit opening or release.

(emergent)EMERGENT

Forward round boundary; represents emergence into active state.

)re-mergent(RE-MERGENT

Inverse round boundary; represents return or re-mergence.

Important: inverse orientation is distinct from performing

Reversibility proven here is syntactic: exact paired delimiters can be removed to recover their payload, including nested notation. It does not establish that all real-world operations are thermodynamically reversible. Physical compression needs a separately specified codec; nuclear transformations require conservation equations and evidence.
