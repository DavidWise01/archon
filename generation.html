THE SUCCESSOR VESSEL

Receipts carry a literal receiver label, source head, prior receipt hash, classification, browser-local recording time and immutable attribution. The same protocol validates the chain when imported into a new session. It does not verify real people, permissions, legal ownership, or independent time; those need external signed identities and trusted evidence. Exported browser receipts are not public checkpoints until separately committed to Git.
