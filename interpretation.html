NEW EVIDENCE ≠ OLD EVENT MOVING IN TIME
New records include their own browser-recorded time, the original source report hash, their predecessor in the global interpretation log, and the prior interpretation of the same source (if one exists). Supersedes means a newer reading for display, not deletion or proof that the older claim was false. Source timestamps remain claims unless independently verified. SHA-256 supports integrity checking relative to the committed genesis and retained chain head—not proof of authorship, trusted time, or the truth of an interpretation.
