## The moth-light

There was a light the size of a firefly, caught between two black trunks.

Dante followed it.

He did not know whether it belonged to a star, a lantern, or something that had learned to shine while buried in the forest. Each time he approached, it drifted farther ahead. Each time he stopped, it waited.

The branches thinned. Wet leaves gave way to stone.

For a moment he saw the slope of a mountain wearing the first gold of morning. The light settled on a rock, pulsed once, and vanished into the rising day.

Dante lifted his foot to climb.

A leopard moved across the path. Farther up, a lion stood in the sun. Behind him came the she-wolf, thin with a hunger that could swallow every road.

The little light had not led him out of danger.

It had led him to the truth of the obstacle.
