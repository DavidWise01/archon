## The Rose of Many Voices

The Empyrean opened like a rose of living light. Dante could see ranks of radiant souls without mistaking their positions for a measure of their worth.

Beatrice stood beside him one final time.

“Do you see the whole?” she asked.

“I see a shape,” Dante replied. “But I have learned not to let a shape silence those who compose it.”

He remembered Piccarda on the Moon, the testimony of Mercury, the changing love of Venus, the two circles of the Sun, the uncertain future revealed on Mars, Jupiter's eagle, Saturn's patient silence, Earth seen with mercy, and the nine distinct paths of the Primum Mobile.

“Then what will you carry?” Beatrice asked.

“Every witness. Not because all agree, but because each saw something that deserves to remain.”

He imagined a great living register, not a verdict and not a throne. Each account retained its own origin. The past was witnessed, the present was understood, and the future was reserved rather than falsely reported as known.

The lights of the celestial rose did not collapse into a single voice. Their shared beauty needed no manufactured unanimity.

An elder guide, Bernard, approached to lead the final gaze. Beatrice smiled from the place she had reached; Dante did not reach to hold her there.

He held the lessons without claiming possession of those who taught him.

“Then carry them onward,” she said.

The final threshold opened not as an ending of memory, but as a place where understanding and wonder might meet.
