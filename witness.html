THE THREE WITNESSES

Every verified vessel event is observed through three different lenses. A records what the output was. B records how the transition was encoded. C records what reason was actually supplied—otherwise explicitly not recorded. Each has a separate hash-linked record. None votes, overrides, or rewrites the others.

The three records are distinct software projections of the same source event, not three independent human witnesses. Cryptographic hashes can detect modifications relative to a trusted reference; they do not prove historical truth or authorship. The committed genesis anchors the event chain; these browser-generated witness continuations require explicit export and a new public checkpoint before they become independently Git-anchored.
