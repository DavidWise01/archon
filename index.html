Historical method / model integrity

Separate the witnesses.

Witness A · Ancient Gnostic material

The Apocryphon of John lists seven powers—Athoth, Eloaios, Astaphaios, Yao, Sabaoth, Adonin, Sabbataios (spellings vary)—and the ruler Yaldabaoth, also called Saklas and Samael. It also mentions seven heavenly kings, five abyssal rulers and a system of 365 angels. Different passages and manuscripts do not reduce to one perfectly uniform hierarchy.

The animal faces and attributes are textual; assigning a particular Dantean vice to each archon is our analogy, not a claim made by that Gnostic document.

Witness B · Dante, a different tradition

Dante's Inferno has nine circles: Limbo; Lust; Gluttony; Avarice; Wrath; Heresy; Violence; Fraud; and Treachery. Its ninth circle is ice, not fire; Lucifer is trapped there. Dante's poem is a medieval Christian literary and moral cosmology, not a disguised Sethian scripture.

We borrow Dante's descent, increasing enclosure and reversal at the bottom, not his exact theology. Gnostic escape normally means movement beyond cosmic ignorance, not traversal of Dante's literal geography.

Witness C · The created 7+1+1 model

1–7: seven limited authorities/filters. 8: the Demiurge's mistaken claim to totality, compared with Dante's fraudulent enclosure. 9: a mirror at the deepest point; the visitor can identify the limit of the system, then reverse direction. This ninth position represents gnosis, not a new ruler, deity or ancient archon.

Our descent is a comparative visualization; the ascent switch reverses the journey to symbolize recognition.

Model safeguards

  • Text: names, forms and sevenfold claims trace to primary-source translations.
  • Analogy: Dante's circles are mapped creatively, not historically equated.
  • Unknown: no single canonical Gnostic planet-to-archon order exists across schools.
  • Geometry: nine nested circles are a navigational graphic, not evidence of physical cosmology.
  • Operation: no external libraries, tracking, network calls or persisted personal data.
