Position 0 — frozen {{i}}

A fixed observer, centered on the seven dynamic concentric terrace fields. The core does not rotate or translate. This is an original model design, not a position in Dante’s text.

Index 0 carries no sin and no archon.

Terraces 1–7

Dante’s seven purgations form a moral ascent. The seven Gnostic powers are mapped here through the seven capital vices from the previous ARCHON version—not because the two traditions historically identified one another.

Positions 8/9 remain labeled soul entry and exit only as reserved placeholders.

Method / observation

Three witnesses, no conflation.

A · Dante

Seven terraces, ordered Pride, Envy, Wrath, Sloth, Avarice, Gluttony, Lust; the corrective habits include humility, charity, gentleness, zeal, generosity, abstinence, chastity.

B · Gnosticism

The Apocryphon of John identifies seven powers ruled by Yaldabaoth. Its archons were not historically assigned to Dante’s later seven deadly sins in this order. The sin-based pairing is an artistic crosswalk.

C · Kernel

{{i}} at 0 is fixed. The seven concentric terraces are animated around it. A selected station changes only the observed level, never the core coordinate.

Future 8 / 9

08 = souls IN; 09 = souls OUT. No souls are observed, processed, classified, transferred or simulated in this version. The Demiurge remains historical context, not an assigned level in this 0–7 coordinate system.
