A · Dante and Virgil

Virgil guides Dante through Hell and up Mount Purgatory before Beatrice becomes his guide. Seven terraces, ordered Pride, Envy, Wrath, Sloth, Avarice, Gluttony, Lust; the corrective habits include humility, charity, gentleness, zeal, generosity, abstinence, chastity.

B · Gnosticism

The Apocryphon of John identifies seven powers ruled by Yaldabaoth. Its archons were not historically assigned to Dante’s later seven deadly sins in this order. The sin-based pairing is an artistic crosswalk.

C · Kernel

Dante is {{i}} at 0, fixed. The seven concentric terraces are animated around it. A selected station changes only the observed level, never the core coordinate.

Future 8 / 9

08 = souls IN; 09 = souls OUT. No souls are observed, processed, classified, transferred or simulated in this version. The Demiurge remains historical context, not an assigned level in this 0–7 coordinate system.
